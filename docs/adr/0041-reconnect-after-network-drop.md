# ADR 0041: Reconnect after a network drop

On the night of 2026-10-13 the Wi-Fi dropped for some minutes. agy reported `Eligibility check failed: … dial tcp: lookup …: no such host`. antiquota did not recognise that as a network failure. Goal turns failed as ordinary errors, used their three retries and stopped. Quota probes read agy's "not signed in" as a real sign-out and walled three healthy accounts for 30 days. The eligibility message also matched the Google verification rule. The owner asked for the Claude Code behaviour: keep the work and continue by itself when the network returns.

`classify_error` now recognises network failures first (`dial tcp`, `no such host`, `network is unreachable` or `down`, `no route to host`, name resolution failures, `i/o timeout`, `TLS handshake timeout`) as `Failure::Offline`, before sign-in, quota and transient words. The agy decoder reports such a turn as `offline`. The turn loop then keeps the account, spends no retry or failover, posts one "waiting to reconnect" notice, shows the session as `offline` ("waiting for network"), and tries the same turn again after 5, 10, 20, 40 and then every 60 seconds, with a prompt that says the network dropped. The first successful attempt posts "Reconnected." Stop still ends the wait. A quota probe that fails offline is unreadable: it neither signs the account out nor parks it for verification. Plain `connection refused` stays a transient error, as the captured contract requires.

Compact transcripts hide the offline turn ends. An engine that the app or the system stops is a separate case: active goals come back paused and need Resume (Slice 25).
